# Prompts to try

Once HoldingsIntel is connected, ask your assistant:

### A stock
- "Is smart money buying NVDA?"
- "Who are the biggest 13F holders of AAPL this quarter?"
- "Any insider buying at Tesla lately?"

### Funds
- "Show me Berkshire Hathaway's full portfolio."
- "Show Berkshire's new and increased positions last quarter."
- "Compare Pershing Square and Third Point: overlap and style."
- "Backtest cloning Pershing Square's top 5 picks."

### Screens
- "Which stocks do all four smart-money signals agree on?"
- "Which funds are doubling down into price weakness?"
- "Which funds moved first into new positions this quarter?"

### People and filings
- "What has Nancy Pelosi been buying recently?"
- "Has any member of Congress bought NVDA?"
- "List the latest activist stakes above 5%."

### Your research lists (with an API key)
- "Add NVDA and Scion Asset Management to my Watchlist."
- "What's on my Watchlist?"
